07Hero Showroom & Vault GroupTwo floors

Hero showroom  ·  customer lounge

Glazed meeting rooms in dark steel frames, services left exposed on track, and a chevron floor mixing timber, charcoal and patterned tile.

A long plan reads as a sequence of rooms rather than a corridor, and the curved glazing at the end gives the floor somewhere to arrive.

08Living spacesTwo houses

No shared vocabulary.

The same three people drew both. A studio with a signature look is really a studio that has stopped listening.

Fluted rosewood, hand-trowelled plaster, green marble underfoot

Three materials and no more.

Restraint is easier to live with than variety.

12Working with usProcess

Four stages. No surprises at the end of any of them.

01

Brief & Site

Measured, studied through the day, signed off before a line is drawn.

02

Planning

Zoning and privacy resolved as diagrams, not as a render asking for approval.

03

Detail & Systems

Joinery, lighting, HVAC and automation drawn together, at scale.

04

Site & Handover

Weekly supervision, snag list closed before handover.

Detailed BOQs are issued at the end of Planning, so you know what every item costs before it is ordered.
